# Auspex review — 2026-06-01

Bug/improvement audit of the Auspex screener + competitive adoption sweep across
the Polymarket tooling and pro-trading-UI landscape. Goal: make the screener
professional and ready for mass use.

> **Provenance & caveat.** Produced by a 23-agent dynamic workflow (6 code-review
> dimensions, each `find → adversarially verify`; 7 competitive web-research
> angles + a design baseline; 3 synthesis agents). An Anthropic **session usage
> limit hit mid-run (17:49)** and killed the 3 synthesis agents and 3 of the 7
> competitive angles. All raw findings, the 4 completed angles, and the baseline
> were recovered from the workflow journal and synthesized here by hand.
> **Update — 2026-06-01:** the 3 angles the limit killed were re-run
> successfully — **PM analytics tools, copy-trade/wallet trackers, and a live

> **Part B (continued)** below. The audit itself is read-only; a separate branch

## Executive summary

Auspex is in good shape: the verifiers found **no fund-loss bugs** and confirmed
the 2026-05-16 money-path fixes held. The code health story is two themes. First,
**the flagship — Trigger Radar — is the biggest liability in the product**: it
shows an "Alerting" state but silently never fires for any market outside the
top-500 snapshot (HIGH), duplicates notifications per tab, grows localStorage
unbounded, and refetches the entire top-500 payload app-wide every 60s. The one
feature no competitor can copy is currently half-wired and quietly lying to users.
Second, **live-tick render cost**: a single WS mid update forces a full screener
re-render *and* a full `react-table` re-sort with zero visible change, and no row
component is memoized — fine at 50 rows, a wall at "mass use" scale.

On competitiveness, Auspex already has a **genuinely professional design system**
(coherent violet/semantic palette, the signature DeltaBar/RcBar/PmBar micro-bars,
a strong a11y baseline, URL-as-state) — better-looking than most Polymarket
third-party tools. The gaps are **screener power-features** (numeric filters,
density/column controls, saved-view presets, keyboard row-nav, a Cmd-K palette —
table-stakes for every pro screener it was benchmarked against) and
**mass-readiness polish** (a fiat on-ramp, real Web Push, a public status page,
resolution-transparency depth that turns its clarity score into the antidote to
Polymarket's #1 user complaint). One embarrassing-but-cheap miss the sweep
corroborated: **the Geist brand font never loads in production** — the whole type
system falls back to system-ui.

**Bottom line on mass-readiness:** the foundation is professional-grade; ship the
Trigger Radar fix + the two cheap perf fixes first (they're correctness/credibility
issues), then the screener power-feature tier, then onboarding/trust. None of it
is a rewrite.

# Part B — Competitive Adoption Roadmap

Benchmarked against **crypto screeners** (Dexscreener, DEXTools, GMGN, Photon,
Birdeye, TradingView), **Polymarket itself** (+ its third-party ecosystem),
**prediction-market rivals** (Kalshi, Manifold, Limitless, Myriad, Drift BET),
and **pro on-chain trading terminals** (Hyperliquid, dYdX v4, GMX, Drift). 61
observations, de-duplicated and grouped; "already-strong" items dropped (listed at
the end as confirmed strengths). Effort: **S**(mall) / **M**(edium) / **L**(arge).

### Convergent headline

Both the analytics and copy-trade angles independently converge on one build:
**turn the leaderboard + follow feed into a risk-scored, labeled copy-trade
surface.** Not raw P&L — realized-vs-unrealized P&L, Sharpe / max-drawdown /
win-rate, behavioral tags (Sharp / Whale / Specialist:Politics / Bot-HFT),
per-category specialization, wash-trade/sybil red-flags, and proportional sizing
+ exit-mirroring on copies. Auspex already owns ~70% of the substrate (wallet
tracker + leaderboard + follow + copy-this-fill), and the Builder Program pays by
attributed volume — so this is the single highest-leverage product line the full
sweep points to.
